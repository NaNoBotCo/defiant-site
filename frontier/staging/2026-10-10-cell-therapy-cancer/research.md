## A. Thai CAR-T: who, since when, how many, outcomes

1. Chulalongkorn University (Faculty of Medicine) with King Chulalongkorn Memorial Hospital (KCMH) runs a point-of-care CD19 CAR-T program on a CliniMACS Prodigy automated closed system, with a lentiviral vector from Miltenyi Biotec · https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13333335 (Mol Ther Oncol, doi 10.1016/j.omton.2026.201278, PubMed 42440458) · 2026-06-17 online, issue 2026-09-17 · high
2. Chula paper: 12 patients with relapsed/refractory large B-cell lymphoma treated January 2020 to December 2025; median age 45.8; manufacturing succeeded in 11, one out-of-specification dose; median vein-to-vein 12 days; CRS in 5 (grade 3 in 1); neurotoxicity in 1 (grade 1) · same URL · 2026-06-17 · high
3. Chula outcomes: abstract gives overall response 58.3% at 3 months with 33.3% complete response; one-year event-free survival 58.3%, overall survival 90%. The discussion section gives a "3-months best ORR of 75%", which conflicts with the abstract; quote the abstract · same URL · 2026-06-17 · high (numbers), medium (which ORR is right)
4. Chula delivered these 12 under a "pilot free-of-charge compassionate access program", funded by a national grant (NESDC, grant N35E690030) and philanthropy, set up to prepare "a streamlined commercial CD19 CAR T cell service". So it is neither a priced hospital service nor a registered trial in the paper's wording · same URL · 2026-06-17 · high
5. Chula cost in the paper: median total USD 111,231 from leukapheresis to day 30 (manufacturing median USD 100,000; leukapheresis USD 858; inpatient USD 7,609; outpatient to day 90 USD 2,764; median stay 27 days). This is provider-perspective cost; patients paid nothing in the pilot. No patient price is published · same URL · 2026-06-17 · high (as cost), low (as a future price)
6. ## D. Thai regulation

25. Thai FDA describes a three-part ATMP system: pre-use authorization of the product, GMP regulation of manufacturing in labs, hospitals and medical schools, and registration as a medicine for commercial sale. Secretary-General Supattra Boonserm, interviewed by PostToday · https://en.fda.moph.go.th/news/road-to-atmps-thai-fda-affirms-thailand-will-not-be-left-behind · 2026-03-09 · high
26. Chula is the first officially certified ATMP research-manufacturing site (GMP for Clinical Research) and recently received GMDP/PIC/S certification for investigational cell products; Siriraj, Ramathibodi and others are applying; about ten companies hold manufacturing licenses and are upgrading to GMP · same URL · 2026-03-09 · high
