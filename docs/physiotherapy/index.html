Physiotherapy & rehab in Thailand

This is the un-flashy one on the wellness list, and maybe the most useful. Physio isn't frontier science — it's established, evidence-based medicine. The difference in Thailand isn't the treatment; it's that you get more of it, at a fraction of the cost, with a therapist who actually has time for you.

Back home, physio is 15 rushed minutes between six other patients, rationed by your insurer. Here it's an hour of one-on-one, daily if you need it, for what a single US session costs — because labour, not quality, is what's cheap.

Not medical advice — Defiant is a concierge, not a clinic. We route to licensed physiotherapists and rehab centres and coordinate the plan with your surgeon.

What it's for

  • Post-surgery rehab — the recovery that decides whether your new knee,

    • hip, or spine surgery actually works. Pair it with the procedure and recover where it's cheap and quiet.

  • Sports & orthopedic injuries — tendons, joints, backs; often alongside

    • Shockwave Therapy for stubborn cases.

  • Neuro rehabilitation — stroke and neurological recovery, where intensive daily

    • therapy (affordable here, rationed at home) genuinely changes outcomes.

  • Chronic pain & mobility — the long, patient work that needs consistent hands-on

    • time.

Why it pairs perfectly with a trip

Every surgery has a recovery tail, and that tail is where physio lives. Come for the procedure, stay on a longer DTV visa if you need weeks of rehab, and recover with daily one-on-one therapy in a Thai hotel instead of fighting your insurer for a third session at home. We build the whole arc — surgery, physio, housing — as one plan.

FAQ

Is physiotherapy in Thailand as good as at home?

The technique is the same evidence-based physiotherapy — the profession is licensed and trained to international standards. What's different is volume and attention: you get more sessions, longer, one-on-one, because it costs a fraction of Western rates. For recovery, more quality therapy time is exactly what improves outcomes.

How much does physio cost compared to the US?

Often a fraction — a full hour of one-on-one therapy for around what a rushed US copay-plus-session runs, without the insurer rationing how many you're "allowed."

Can I do rehab after surgery I had at home?

Yes — many people fly in specifically for intensive rehab after a surgery done elsewhere. We coordinate with your surgeon's protocol and build a daily plan.

How long should I plan to stay for rehab?

Depends on the case — a knee might want a few weeks, neuro rehab longer. For longer courses we set up the DTV visa so you can stay as long as recovery needs.
