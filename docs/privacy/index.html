Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 2025 Organization: Defiant Health

This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, and protect information. By accessing or using our services, you agree to the practices described herein.

1. Data Collection and Use

We may collect limited, aggregated, and non-identifiable information for the purposes of analytics, site optimization, and marketing. We do not sell, lease, or otherwise transfer personal information to third parties.

2. Personal Information and U.S. Government Requests

We will not share, disclose, or otherwise provide any personal information that could reasonably be considered subject to the Privacy Act of 1974 (5 U.S.C. § 552a) with any United States Government agency under any circumstances. This prohibition applies regardless of administration, jurisdiction, or request type. Any governmental demand for data will be resisted and challenged through all available legal means.

3. Medical Information and HIPAA Disclaimer

We are not a covered entity or business associate under the Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act of 1996 (“HIPAA”). Users must not submit protected health information (PHI) or medical records to us. Any such information received will not be treated as HIPAA-covered data.

4. Security

We implement reasonable technical and organizational measures to safeguard data against unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction. However, users should assume that network infrastructure outside of our direct control may contain vulnerabilities. For sensitive communications, end-to-end encrypted channels are strongly recommended. Our designated secure contact is ProtonMail — the address is available via the reveal button on the defiant.to contact page (we don't print it where bots can harvest it) — but users are advised to exercise caution even when using encrypted services.

5. User Rights

You may request clarification regarding the nature of the information we collect and its uses. We will respond in accordance with applicable law.

6. Changes to This Policy

We reserve the right to amend or update this Privacy Policy at any time. Updates will be posted with a revised “last updated” date.
