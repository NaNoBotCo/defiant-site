Health Checkup Packages in Chiang Mai: What ฿ Buys You

Thailand treats the annual checkup as a retail product, and it's one of the country's best ideas. Private hospitals publish package menus the way restaurants publish set lunches: named tiers, listed inclusions, posted prices, same-week appointments. No referral, no prior authorization, no mystery bill four months later. For the price of a US copay-and-deductible adventure, you leave with a bound English report and every number that matters.

The tiers, decoded

Package medicine has one caveat worth saying plainly: screening menus love tests that photograph well. A checkup is a snapshot, not a diagnosis, and a good physician conversation about the results — included at every hospital here — is the part that turns numbers into decisions. Not medical advice; bring your own doctor into the loop for what to screen and how often.

How the day actually goes

1. Book a day or two ahead — phone, LINE, or walk in and pick a morning. Fast overnight (usually 8–12 hours; water is fine) for the blood panel. 2. Arrive early, wear two-piece clothing, bring your passport and any home lab results for comparison. 3. The circuit takes two to four hours — stations, not waiting rooms. Most results land the same day; imaging reads may take another day. 4. Ask for the English report when you book, not after. Every hospital above produces one; saying it early makes it seamless. 5. The physician readout is included. Bring questions. This is also the cheapest specialist referral system on earth — anything flagged gets a same-building follow-up at Chiang Mai prices.

FAQ

How much is a full health checkup in Chiang Mai?

Indicative August 2026: basic packages ฿2,500–5,000, standard ฿5,000–10,000, executive ฿10,000–25,000. The menu is posted; the price is the price.

Do I need an appointment for a health checkup in Thailand?

A day or two ahead is plenty at most hospitals; some take same-day walk-ins if you've fasted. Booking gets you the English report and a smoother morning.

Will the results be in English?

Yes — ask when you book. The bound English report is standard product at Chiang Mai's private hospitals and travels well to doctors at home.

How long does a hospital checkup take?

Two to four hours for the circuit, with most results the same day and the physician readout included before you leave.
