LGBTQIA+ Community Flags and Their Meanings

1. Progress Pride Flag

  • Combines the rainbow flag with chevrons for trans (blue, pink, white), Black and Brown stripes for people of color.
  • Designed by Daniel Quasar (2018).
  • Meaning: Inclusion of trans and nonwhite queer people, highlighting intersectionality.

2. Rainbow Pride Flag

  • Original LGBTQ+ pride flag, created by Gilbert Baker (1978).
  • Current six-stripe version: red (life), orange (healing), yellow (sunlight), green (nature), blue (serenity), violet (spirit).
  • Widely recognized symbol of LGBTQ+ pride.

3. Nonbinary Pride Flag

  • Yellow, white, purple, black.
  • Created by Kye Rowan (2014).
  • Meaning:
  • Yellow: outside the gender binary
  • White: many or all genders
  • Purple: mix of male and female
  • Black: no gender

4. Genderfluid Pride Flag

  • Pink, white, purple, black, blue stripes.
  • Created by JJ Poole (2012).
  • Meaning:
  • Pink: femininity
  • White: all genders
  • Purple: mix of masculinity/femininity
  • Black: lack of gender
  • Blue: masculinity

5. Transgender Pride Flag

  • Blue, pink, white stripes.
  • Created by Monica Helms (1999).
  • Meaning:
  • Blue: traditional male
  • Pink: traditional female
  • White: nonbinary, transitioning, or undefined

6. Lesbian Pride Flag (Orange-Pink 7-Stripe Version)

  • Orange to pink gradient.
  • Updated design (2018) is most common.
  • Meaning:
  • Dark orange: gender nonconformity
  • Orange: independence
  • Light orange: community
  • White: unique relationships to womanhood
  • Pink: serenity/peace
  • Dusty rose: love/sex
  • Dark rose: femininity

7. Agender Pride Flag

  • Black, gray, white, green.
  • Created by Salem X (2014).
  • Meaning:
  • Black/white: absence of gender
  • Gray: semi-gender or partial gender
  • Green: nonbinary

8. Bisexual Pride Flag

  • Pink, purple, blue.
  • Created by Michael Page (1998).
  • Meaning:
  • Pink: attraction to same gender
  • Blue: attraction to other genders
  • Purple: overlap/both

9. Pansexual Pride Flag

  • Pink, yellow, blue.
  • Circulating since ~2010.
  • Meaning:
  • Pink: attraction to women
  • Yellow: attraction regardless of gender (nonbinary)
  • Blue: attraction to men

10. Polysexual Pride Flag

  • Pink, green, blue.
  • Created by Tumblr user “Samlin” (2012).
  • Meaning:
  • Pink: attraction to women
  • Green: attraction to nonbinary
  • Blue: attraction to men
