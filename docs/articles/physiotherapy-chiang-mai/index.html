Physical Therapy in Chiang Mai: The Clinic Map and the Session Math

Rehab is arithmetic. A knee replacement wants a course of sessions, not a session; a shoulder wants months of them. At US clinic rates that arithmetic quietly amputates the back half of most recovery plans — people stop going when the copays outrun the progress. Chiang Mai's quiet superpower is that the arithmetic works: session rates low enough to finish the course, with unhurried one-on-one hours the Western clinic model can't afford anymore.

The physiotherapy overview makes the whole-country case. This page is the Chiang Mai working sheet: the clinic map and the session math.

Three doors, three flavors:

  • Hospital physio departments — machine-rich (ultrasound, shockwave, hydrotherapy at the big houses), surgeon in the same building, records in one file. The natural home for post-surgical courses; every hospital on the hospital guide runs one.
  • Standalone clinics — the mapped rows above; licensed physios (นักกายภาพบำบัด, nák gaai-yá-phâap bam-bàt — physical therapist), longer hours, easier walk-in rhythm, often stronger English in the tourist-adjacent blocks.
  • Home visits — a physio comes to the recovery apartment with a folding table. For the first weeks after joint work, this is the difference between doing the exercises and describing them.

How the sequence works after surgery

1. The surgeon writes the plan — frequency, restrictions, milestones. If the operation happened here, the physio department is downstairs and the referral is same-day. 2. Front-load the supervised weeks. Daily or near-daily early sessions beat weekly heroics; this is where Chiang Mai rates change what's possible. 3. Graduate to a program — clinics here are generous with written home programs and video check-ins once you've earned independence. 4. Flying home mid-course? Leave with the progress notes in English and the exercise program documented — the hand-off is a paperwork problem, and paperwork problems are solvable ones.

For the recovery weeks themselves: massage is the reward, physio is the plan — in that order, with the surgeon's blessing on both.

FAQ

How much does physiotherapy cost in Chiang Mai?

Indicative August 2026: ฿600–1,500 per one-on-one session depending on setting, with course packages commonly discounted. A full 20-session post-op course runs around ฿15,000–25,000.

Do I need a referral for physio in Thailand?

Standalone clinics take direct bookings for most musculoskeletal work. Post-surgical courses should run on the surgeon's written plan — which any hospital here produces as a matter of routine.

Do Chiang Mai physiotherapists speak English?

At hospital departments and the established standalone clinics, working English is the norm, and demonstration carries the rest. Say what hurts; point; they take it from there.

Can I do surgery and rehab in one Thailand trip?

That's the model at its best — operate, then spend the supervised weeks recovering at rates that let you finish the course. The concierge desk sequences exactly this.
