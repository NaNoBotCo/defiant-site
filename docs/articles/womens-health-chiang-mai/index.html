Women's Health in Chiang Mai: Mapped, Phoned, and Priced

Finding a gynecologist in a new country is a chore layered on top of something personal, and most of what the internet offers is a forum thread from 2019. Chiang Mai deserves better coverage than that: the city has a deep bench of OB-GYNs, hospital women's centers with modern screening equipment, university faculty, and the standing Thai custom that makes all of it approachable — walk-in clinics, posted prices, and no gatekeeping referral maze between you and a specialist.

The three ways in

  • Hospital women's health centers — the private hospitals on the hospital guide all run dedicated centers: imaging and labs down the hall, English at the desk, appointments or walk-ins. The most comfortable first stop if you're new.
  • OB-GYN clinics — Thailand's evening institution: hospital specialists (สูตินรีแพทย์, sǔu-tì-ná-rii-phâet — obstetrician-gynecologist) seeing patients at their own storefront clinics after hospital hours. Shorter waits, gentler prices, the same physician you'd meet at the hospital. The mapped rows above are full of them.
  • The university system — Maharaj (Suan Dok) and Sriphat carry the deepest sub-specialty bench in the North: gynecologic oncology, urogynecology, fertility medicine. This is where complex things get answered.

Asking for what you want is normal here

Three requests that are entirely ordinary in Thai clinics, stated in one sentence at the desk:

  • A female physician — common, and commonly requested; hospital desks will tell you which sessions.
  • A chaperone in the room, or your partner with you — routine either way.
  • English-language results — ask at booking; every private facility above produces them.

Thai clinical style tends brisk and unembarrassed — screening questions arrive without ceremony, and it reads as efficiency, not judgment. Bring your history written down (dates, medications by generic name, prior results) and the visit runs beautifully.

FAQ

How do I find an English-speaking gynecologist in Chiang Mai?

Start at a private hospital women's center — English at the desk is standard — or pick a mapped clinic above and ask when booking. Requesting a female, English-speaking physician is a normal one-sentence ask.

How much does a gynecologist visit cost in Chiang Mai?

Indicative August 2026: ฿500–1,500 for the consult, with ultrasound ฿800–2,500 and screening bundles from about ฿1,000. Posted prices, no referral required.

Do I need a referral to see a specialist?

No — Thailand runs on direct access. Walk into a hospital women's center or an OB-GYN's evening clinic and book the specialist directly.

Can I get my results in English?

Yes — ask at booking rather than after. Private hospitals and established clinics produce English reports that travel well to doctors anywhere.
