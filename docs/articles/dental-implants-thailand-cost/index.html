Dental Implants in Thailand: Real Prices, Real Timelines

Dental implants are the procedure that built Thai medical tourism, and the arithmetic hasn't changed: the same titanium, the same brand catalogs, a dental surgeon who places hundreds a year, and a bill that reads like a typo if you've priced the work in the United States. What has changed is how much nonsense now ranks above the actual numbers. So: the actual numbers, the actual timeline, and the questions that keep a quote real.

The timeline nobody puts in the headline

A standard implant is two trips, not one:

1. Trip one — placement. Assessment, 3D imaging, extraction if needed, and the titanium post goes in. A few days in the chair spread over a week or so. 2. The quiet months. Osseointegration — the bone knitting to the post — takes three to six months. You do this part at home, or on a beach; the implant doesn't care. 3. Trip two — the crown. Abutment and crown fitted, bite tuned. Usually under a week.

Single-visit "immediate load" cases exist — good bone, favorable position, and a dentist willing to say so in writing. Treat one-trip promises for ordinary cases the way you'd treat any headline that skips the middle of the story.

Full-arch work (All-on-4, All-on-6) compresses differently: a provisional bridge often goes in fast, with the definitive bridge on a return visit. That's exactly the return-trip rhythm the follow-up guide is about — and why the DTV's five years of multi-entry runway pairs so well with dental work.

What keeps a quote real

Get all four in writing before the flight — this is the whole method, and clinics that do good work answer these happily:

  • The implant line by name. Straumann, Nobel Biocare, Osstem — brand and model belong on the quote, because they're what any dentist at home will service later.
  • Who places it. The dental surgeon's name and how many implants they place a year.
  • What the number includes. Imaging, surgical guide, abutment, crown material, bite adjustments — the unbundled extras are where soft quotes hide.
  • The warranty, portably. What's covered, for how long, and what documentation travels home with you.

The concierge desk does this collection as a matter of routine, and how you're protected explains what the paper trail is for.

Bangkok has the giant dedicated centers; Chiang Mai does the same catalog at Chiang Mai overheads — hospital dental departments at the private hospitals and standalone implant clinics around the city. The right room depends on your case complexity, your calendar, and where you'd rather spend the quiet months in between.

FAQ

How much do dental implants cost in Thailand?

Indicative 2026 range: $1,000–1,800 per tooth complete (post, abutment, crown) versus $3,000–5,000 in the US. Full-arch cases quote per jaw and still commonly land under a single US implant-and-crown sequence.

How long do I need to stay in Thailand for an implant?

About a week for placement, then three to six months of healing wherever you like, then under a week for the crown. Two trips is the standard shape.

Are Thai dental implants the same brands as in the US?

The major clinics quote the same global catalogs — Straumann, Nobel Biocare, Osstem. That's why the brand belongs on your written quote: any dentist at home can service a known system.

Is it safe to get dental implants in Thailand?

Implantology is mature, high-volume work here. The safety question is really a selection question — named surgeon, named system, written plan — and that's a solvable one.
