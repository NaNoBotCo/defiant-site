Thailand Emergency Numbers: The One-Page Card That Works

Thailand does not use 911, and the number that matters most — 1669, the national medical emergency line — is one most visitors have never seen written down. This page is the card. Save the share image, screenshot the tables, send it to the person you travel with. It's built to be useful before you ever need it, which is the only good time.

National numbers, verified 2026-08-17. Save 1669 before you need it.

1669 dispatches free public ambulances nationwide, 24 hours. The operators work Thai-first: speak slowly, lead with where you are — hotel name, road, district — and hand the phone to any Thai speaker nearby if you can. Hotel front desks make this call flawlessly; "call 1669 for me" is a complete sentence.

1155, the tourist police, answer in English around the clock and can conference-bridge you to the right service when you're not sure what you need. That's their whole job, and they're good at it.

The quiet preparation that pays

  • Put 1669 and your hotel's address in your phone tonight. In Thai script too, if you can — screenshots of the hotel's LINE location pin work.
  • Carry your insurance card and one photo ID. Private ERs move faster when the billing question answers itself; how you're protected explains the paperwork trail.
  • US veterans: ER care abroad can be reimbursable under the VA Foreign Medical Program — the FMP page walks the claim.
  • Embassies come later. Medical first, then your embassy's citizen services if you're admitted; they help with notification and records, not treatment.

FAQ

What is the emergency number in Thailand?

1669 for medical emergencies and ambulances, nationwide and free. 191 is police, 199 is fire, 1155 is tourist police in English.

Do Thai ambulances cost money?

The public 1669 service is free. Private hospital ambulances bill like the private hospitals they belong to — real but published numbers, usually settled with the ER bill.

Will the 1669 operator speak English?

Assume Thai-first. Lead with your location, speak slowly, or have any Thai speaker — hotel desk, taxi driver, neighbor — place the call. 1155 (tourist police) bridges in English when you're stuck.
