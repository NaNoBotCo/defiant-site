FAQ

Is regenerative medicine in Thailand legal and safe?

Yes — Thailand's Ministry of Public Health regulates it, and reputable hospitals work within that framework. "Safe" depends entirely on the facility: our rule is a regulated hospital or licensed clinic, a named physician, and a published protocol. We blacklist anyone promising a cure, and so should you.

Does "not FDA-approved" mean it doesn't work?

No. The FDA is famously slow and cautious, and plenty of treatments with real mechanisms and solid overseas track records aren't approved in the US — for regulatory and commercial reasons, not because they fail. That said, "early evidence" is real too: we tell you which bucket each treatment is in so you're deciding with open eyes, not marketing.

How much does longevity treatment cost versus the US?

Typically 20–40% of US or where-it's-even-available pricing, and often the US doesn't offer it at all. Every program is quoted in writing before you commit.

Can I combine longevity treatments with a trip I'm already taking?

Absolutely — many clients stack a regenerative program onto a surgery recovery, a glowup, or a longer DTV visa stay. We build the schedule around your main reason for coming.
