Sprucing up the undercarriage

Nobody back home will give you a straight, un-embarrassed conversation about pelvic floor, incontinence, or sexual function — and if they do, the treatments cost a fortune or aren't offered at all. Thailand treats this like any other wellness service: matter-of-fact, private, affordable. Here's the no-nonsense menu and where the evidence actually stands.

Not medical advice — Defiant is a concierge, not a clinic. This is grown-up information. We route to licensed medical facilities with real clinicians, and treat your privacy as the whole point.

The menu

  • Emsella — the "EMS chair" — Evidence: solid for incontinence. You sit

    • (fully clothed) on a chair that fires electromagnetic pulses to contract your pelvic-floor muscles thousands of times per session. FDA-cleared for urinary incontinence, and increasingly used for pelvic-floor strength and sexual wellness. Zero downtime, no anesthesia — the most established thing on this list.

  • Low-intensity shockwave (Li-ESWT) for erectile function — *Evidence:

  • PRP shots — the P-shot & O-shot — Evidence: early. Your own platelet-rich

    • plasma injected for sexual wellness (see PRP & growth factors). Popular, plausible, evidence still thin — real, not miraculous.

Why Thailand

The US either won't discuss it, doesn't offer it, or charges specialist prices with a side of shame. Thailand's private clinics do this work routinely, discreetly, and at a fraction of the cost — and we make sure it's a licensed medical facility, not a spa with a machine it doesn't understand.

FAQ

Does the Emsella chair actually work?

For urinary incontinence and pelvic-floor strength, yes — it's FDA-cleared and backed by real evidence, and it's completely non-invasive (you stay clothed, there's no downtime). Its use for sexual wellness is newer but built on the same real mechanism.

Is shockwave for ED legitimate?

It's emerging — promising trials and a real mechanism (improving blood flow), but not FDA-approved for ED, so it's used off-label. That's the FDA being slow, not proof it fails; it's also not a guarantee. We'll tell you exactly where the evidence sits so you choose knowingly.

Is any of this awkward or invasive?

Emsella is done fully clothed, sitting in a chair. The others are quick clinic procedures. Thai clinics handle this discreetly and without judgment — genuinely one of the easier things to get done here. Your privacy is the service.

How much does it cost compared to the US?

A fraction — and several of these the US barely offers. Emsella sessions, shockwave courses, and PRP shots are all quoted in writing before you commit.
