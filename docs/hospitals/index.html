Rajavej Hospital

Chiang MaiThai HAPrivate general hospital

Rajavej Hospital (โรงพยาบาลราชเวชเชียงใหม่) is a private general hospital in Chiang Mai. We list it for one specific, essential reason: **it is, in our experience, the best hospital in Chiang Mai for US veterans using the VA Foreign Medical Program.** If you're a vet, that single fact outweighs a lot of brochure shine.
