The science, graded

Graded: promising-to-mixed for orthopedic indications (knee osteoarthritis has real trials with modest effects), experimental for autoimmune conditions, and unproven for anti-aging and most of what Instagram sells. Cell source, handling and dose vary wildly between clinics — that variance, not the concept, is the risk.

The Defiant take

Our rule: regulated facility, named physician, published protocol, no cure-words. A clinic that promises to cure ALS, autism or aging gets blacklisted — by us and by you.
