Skin tightening — the deepest menu in Asia

Thailand offers more skin-tightening modalities than almost anywhere, and they aren't interchangeable:

  • HIFU (Ultherapy and generics) — focused ultrasound, tightens deep, no downtime.
  • RF (radiofrequency) — heat-based: Thermage (one big session), Morpheus8

    • and RF-microneedling (tightening plus texture, minimal downtime), Sofwave and newer devices.

  • Thread lifts (PDO) — a mechanical, non-surgical lift under the skin.

Which one depends on your skin, budget, and how much (if any) downtime you'll accept. We match the device to the face, not the marketing — and none of it needs a recovery week. Not enough lift? That's when a surgical facelift enters the conversation.

Tattoo removal

Pico laser shatters ink into particles your body clears — all colours, including the stubborn blues and greens older lasers couldn't touch. It takes several sessions spaced weeks apart, so it suits repeat trips or a longer stay. Thailand's per-session price is a fraction of US laser-clinic rates.

The one rule: genuine product, licensed clinic

Counterfeit Botox and filler are a real problem worldwide, and injectables are only as safe as what's actually in the syringe. Our rule is simple: hospital dermatology departments and licensed clinics only, genuine product with batch numbers on the receipt. It's the boring, correct answer, and it's what keeps a glowup a glowup instead of an ER visit.

FAQ

Can I really do these while recovering from surgery?

Many of them, yes — that's the whole appeal. Non-invasive treatments like Pico laser, HIFU, and skin boosters have near-zero downtime and can run on a schedule your surgeon approves, while a bigger procedure heals. Injectables near a surgical site are spaced out; we plan the timing with your clinician so nothing interferes.

Is Botox and filler in Thailand genuine and safe?

At a hospital dermatology department or a licensed clinic, yes — genuine, doctor- administered, with batch numbers on the receipt. The counterfeit-injectable problem is global, not Thai; the answer everywhere is to use a real medical facility, which is exactly where we route you.

How much can I fit into one trip?

A lot — a single week can hold Pico, HIFU, a skin-booster session, and injectables, because none of them need real recovery. The limit is usually spacing injectables correctly, not downtime. We map a schedule so everything lands and heals in your window.

What's the difference between HIFU, a thread lift, and a facelift?

Think of it as a ladder. HIFU tightens with ultrasound (no cutting, subtle, repeatable). A PDO thread lift gives a slightly stronger, still-non-surgical lift for 1–2 years. A surgical facelift repositions the deep tissue for a 10–15-year result. Many people climb the ladder — start with HIFU and threads, move to surgery years later.

What's the best skin-tightening treatment?

There isn't one "best" — it depends on your skin and how much downtime you'll take. HIFU and Thermage tighten deep with zero downtime; Morpheus8 and RF-microneedling add texture and take a few days; thread lifts give a mechanical lift. We match the device to your face rather than to whatever a clinic is promoting this month.

Does tattoo removal really get all the ink out?

Modern Pico lasers clear most tattoos, including the colours older lasers struggled with, over several sessions spaced weeks apart. Full clearance depends on the ink, age, and colours — we set clear expectations before you start, not after.

How much do these cost compared to the US?

Typically 40–70% less, sometimes more — a Botox session that's $150–500 here runs $400–1,500 in a US clinic; a HIFU treatment a fraction of the US price. All indicative and confirmed in writing before you book.
