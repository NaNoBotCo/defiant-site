CBT in Chiang Mai — talk therapy you can actually book

Cognitive behavioural therapy is the most-studied talk therapy there is, and the question people actually type — is there an English-speaking therapist in Chiang Mai? — has a better answer than the forums suggest. This page is the map of who states CBT on their own page, read from each provider's own site on 2026-08-26, with the source attached. Nobody is ranked and nobody paid to be here.

Not medical advice — Defiant is a concierge, not a clinic. Mood is serious; this is information about which providers exist and what they say about themselves, not treatment and not a recommendation of any medication or against one.

The words to know

การบำบัดทางความคิดและพฤติกรรม (kan bambat thang khwam-khit lae phruettikam — treatment by thought and behaviour) is CBT; on clinic pages it often just appears as "CBT". Two titles readers conflate: a จิตแพทย์ (chit-ta-phaet, psychiatrist) is the medical doctor who can prescribe; a นักจิตวิทยา (nak-chit-ta-witthaya, psychologist) or counsellor is who sits with you for the weekly talk-therapy work. Many people use both; they are different desks.

Who states CBT, on their own page

Counselling Thailand — English-first, prices published

A private practice at 53 Chonpratan Road, Suthep, working in English and Thai, whose own page lists CBT among its methods and — rare in this trade — publishes its prices: 2,675 baht for 60 minutes, 3,745 baht for 90 (its page's figures; confirm when you book). Monday–Saturday 8am–8pm, in person or online.

  • Phone / WhatsApp: 098-776-8857 · +66 98 776 8857
  • Source: counsellingthailand.com/therapy-chiang-mai

Chiang Mai Holistic — the multilingual centre

A therapy centre at 17/8 Canal Road, Suthep, listing CBT among its modalities, with bilingual English–Thai therapists in person and more languages online (Mandarin, Malay, Indonesian, Tagalog, Hindi). Monday–Saturday 9am–9pm, Sunday from noon. No prices published.

  • Contact: info@chiangmaiholistic.com · WhatsApp +66 83 248 1250
  • Source: chiangmaiholistic.com

Counseling Chiang Mai — online-first

States CBT, DBT, ACT and mindfulness-based approaches in English and Thai, with a free consultation call first. It publishes no street address or phone — contact runs through its site form, sessions in person "in a private setting" or online.

  • Source: chiangmaicounseling.com

Suan Prung Psychiatric Hospital (โรงพยาบาลสวนปรุง) — the public anchor

The Department of Mental Health's regional psychiatric hospital, and the one public institution whose own service page names การบำบัดทางความคิดและพฤติกรรม — CBT — inside its psychiatric home-ward programme. It also states a child-and-adolescent clinic (ages 12–18, Mon/Wed/Fri 08:00–16:00, Tue mornings) and a counselling service run by psychiatric nurses. Working language is Thai; bring a Thai speaker or ask Defiant to sit with you.

  • Phone: 053-908500 · counselling service ext. 60317, 60323
  • Source: suanprung.go.th

Nakornping Hospital (โรงพยาบาลนครพิงค์) — the public psychiatric clinic

The provincial public hospital's own clinic page states a full psychiatric outpatient clinic — depression, anxiety, insomnia, mood disorders — staffed by psychiatrists and clinical psychologists, in building 16 (the blue building), floor 1, Mon–Fri 08:00–16:00. Its page names the clinic and the psychologists but not CBT itself, so we print exactly that much. Public-hospital pricing and queues; Thai-first.

  • Phone: 053-999200 ext. 2801, 2809
  • Source: nkp-hospital.go.th

How to choose, plainly

  • You want weekly English-language CBT: the three private practices above

    • are the ones that say so themselves. Ask each the same two questions: is my therapist trained in CBT specifically, and what does a course of 8–12 sessions cost. A practice that publishes prices has already answered half.

  • You may also need prescriptions (or an evaluation): that is a

    • psychiatrist — Nakornping's clinic, Suan Prung, or a private จิตแพทย์ clinic. Therapy and prescribing can run in parallel at different desks.

  • Budget matters: public hospitals cost a fraction of private sessions;

    • the trade is queues and Thai as the working language.

  • If substances are part of the picture: CBT is a core tool there too,

    • and both public hospitals above state substance-treatment clinics on the same pages. The places for that work are mapped on the addiction page.

  • Depression that has not moved with talk or medication: TMS is

    • established, FDA-approved psychiatry and Thailand does it well — that story is on the brain-stimulation page.

The checklist before the first session

1. Ask the CBT question directly — มีบริการบำบัดแบบ CBT ไหม (mi borikan bambat baep CBT mai) — do you offer CBT? A clinic that says จิตเวช (psychiatry) has not necessarily said talk therapy. 2. Bring your story on paper if you have one: prior diagnoses, what you have tried, current medication by molecule name — the same lesson as our estradiol page. 3. Itemised receipts — แยกรายการ (yaek rai kan) — every session, if any insurer or the VA will ever see this. Veterans: FMP reimburses service-connected care abroad; the walkthrough is =VA-FMP. 4. Inference — teletherapy across borders is an ordinary thing in this trade now; every practice above offers online sessions, so a first video call before you commit to a therapist is a reasonable ask anywhere.
