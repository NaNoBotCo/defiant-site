What it costs. All of it.

In America, you can't get a price. You call the hospital, you ask what a knee costs, and nobody — not the front desk, not billing, not your own insurer — will give you a straight number until the bill arrives and it's too late to argue.

Thailand is the opposite. Clinics post their prices on the wall. Hospitals publish rate cards. They run promotions and openly undercut each other, because here, price is something you're allowed to know. That transparency is the whole reason care costs a fraction of what it does back home — and it's the thing Defiant is built around.

So here's our half of the deal, in the same spirit: exactly what we charge, in public, with no markup hidden inside a "package."

How we price — radical transparency

  • You always see the clinic's posted price. We gather actual quotes from multiple clinics so you watch the price battle happen — we don't bury it.
  • Our fee is a flat, published number — not a cut of your procedure. We don't make more when you spend more, so we have zero reason to steer you to the expensive room. We route you to the best clinic, not the highest-paying one.
  • If a clinic pays us a referral fee, that's their cost, not added to yours — and we'll tell you which ones do.

That's the entire pitch: the straight-dealing broker America won't give you, in the one country that already runs on posted prices.

What you're actually buying

This whole website is free, and we mean it — the prices, the guides, the VA walkthrough. So be clear about what the fee buys, because it's only two things a website physically cannot do:

  • A trained medical interpreter beside you at every appointment. Not a hotel concierge, not a nephew who "speaks some English" — a professional, so nothing about your body is left to Google Translate or a nod-and-smile.
  • Someone who has run this trip a hundred times, handling the parts that go sideways. The surgeon who reschedules, the records that didn't transfer, the pharmacy that's out of your dose, the 6 a.m. ride to pre-op. You feel the value of this exactly when something breaks.

Everything below is logistics wrapped around those two.

How a Defiant trip actually goes

No black box. Start to finish, here's the shape of it:

  • 1 · The call. A free intake — you tell us the procedure, the timing, the worries. We tell you, straight, whether Thailand is even the right call for you (sometimes it isn't, and we'll say so).
  • 2 · The quotes. We gather real, written prices from several vetted clinics for your case and lay them side by side — you watch the price battle instead of taking our word for it.
  • 3 · The plan. Appointments booked, medical records moved and translated, and the travel piece sorted — including whether a long-stay DTV visa fits if you'll need real recovery time.
  • 4 · You land, and you're met. Airport pickup, a room set up near the hospital, a local number that reaches a human.
  • 5 · The consult — with your interpreter in the room. Every appointment, someone trained making sure the surgeon hears exactly what you mean and you hear exactly what they mean.
  • 6 · The procedure, and the room. If you're admitted, this is the part people come home surprised by: your own room, often with a view, staff bringing food through the day, a nurse call answered in minutes rather than hours. We're reachable the whole time.
  • 7 · Recovery. Check-ins, medication runs, errands, a companion if you want one — the unglamorous week that a website can't sit with you through.
  • 8 · Home, and after. Follow-up once you're back, itemized English billing for your insurer or VA claim, and a hand-off to a provider where you live. You're looked after the whole way — and if you ever want the full picture of how that works, here's how you're protected.
