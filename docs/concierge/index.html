What it costs. All of it.

In America, you can't get a price. You call the hospital, you ask what a knee costs, and nobody — not the front desk, not billing, not your own insurer — will give you a straight number until the bill arrives and it's too late to argue.

Thailand is the opposite. Clinics post their prices on the wall. Hospitals publish rate cards. They run promotions and openly undercut each other, because here, price is something you're allowed to know. That transparency is the whole reason care costs a fraction of what it does back home — and it's the thing Defiant is built around.

So here's our half of the deal, in the same spirit: exactly what we charge, in public, with no markup hidden inside a "package."

How we price — radical transparency

  • You always see the clinic's real price. We gather actual quotes from multiple

    • clinics so you watch the price battle happen. We don't bury it.

  • Our fee is a flat, published number — not a cut of your procedure. We don't

    • make more when you spend more, so we have zero reason to steer you to the expensive room. We route you to the best clinic, not the highest-paying one.

  • If a clinic pays us a referral fee, that's their cost, not added to yours —

    • and we'll tell you which ones do.

That's the entire pitch: the straight-dealing broker America won't give you, in the one country that already runs on posted prices.
